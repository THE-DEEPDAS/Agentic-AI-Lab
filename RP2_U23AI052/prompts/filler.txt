Archive note 1. The municipal library catalogue was reorganised during the spring, and the reading room moved to the second floor. Opening hours were extended on weekday evenings, and the periodicals section now keeps the last six months of issues on open shelves. Volunteers help visitors with the self-service lending machines.

Archive note 2. A community garden committee meets on the first Monday of each month. Members rotate responsibility for watering, composting and tool storage. Seed swaps take place twice a year, and surplus vegetables are shared with the neighbourhood kitchen. New members are welcome after a short orientation walk.

Archive note 3. The regional orchestra rehearses in the old town hall. The programme for the season includes two symphonies, a choral work and a chamber evening. Tickets are sold in advance at the box office, and a limited number of standing places is released on the day of each concert.

Archive note 4. A maintenance bulletin for the bicycle workshop lists the tools available to members: torque wrenches, truing stands, chain checkers and a parts washer. Members are asked to record repairs in the logbook and to return borrowed tools before closing time.

Archive note 5. The school science fair displayed projects on water filtration, plant growth under coloured light and simple electric circuits. Judges praised the clear posters and the careful recording of measurements. Certificates were handed out at the closing assembly.

Archive note 6. The harbour authority published a timetable of ferry crossings for the summer period. Additional sailings run on weekends, and a small cafe on the upper deck serves hot drinks. Passengers with bicycles board first, followed by foot passengers and then vehicles.

Archive note 7. A local history society collected photographs of the market square across several decades. Captions describe the shops, the tram line and the fountain that was replaced after repairs. The collection is stored in acid-free folders and can be viewed by appointment.

Archive note 8. The swimming club published its training plan for the winter term, with sessions for beginners, intermediate swimmers and a masters group. Lane allocation is posted at the pool entrance, and parents are asked to arrive ten minutes early to help with equipment.
